How borrowing oil from the reserve can make money

In 2026 the government lent out oil from its emergency reserve. Companies have to give the oil back later, plus extra. Here is how that can still pay off, using one barrel.

  1. Borrow a barrelThe government lends you oil from the reserve.
  2. Sell it nowOil is scarce, so today's price is high.
  3. Lock in a later priceThe same day, you sign a deal to buy oil in a year or more at a price set today. Later oil costs less.
  4. Give it back, plus extraYou return the barrel and the extra oil the government asked for. You keep what is left.
