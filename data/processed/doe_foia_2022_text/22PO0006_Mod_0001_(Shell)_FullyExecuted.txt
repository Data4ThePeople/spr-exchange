14. DESCRIPTION OF AMENDMENT/MODIFICATION (Organized by UCF section headings, including solicitation/contract subject matter where feasible.)
Paragraphs 2, "Custody Transfer," and 3, "Term" of the above numbered exchange agreement are supplemented as follows:
350,000 barrels awarded under Exchange Agreement No. 22PO0006 at Big Hill to be delivered in April 2022, will now be delivered from West
Hackberry in the month of April 2022 via the Lake Charles Meter Station DLI. STUSCO should take delivery of 350MB no later than April 30,
2022 on a best efforts basis, in a single cargo of 350MB. If delivery cannot be completed by April 30, 2022, then this volume will be considered
undeliverable. Custody transfer quantity and quality measurements will be the DOE LCMS meters and in-line sampler for deliveries into the
Zydeco-Shell 22" pipeline. Secondary measurement for quantity will be at the West Hackberry meter and quality based on grab samples taken at
the DOE Lake Charles Meter Station. The API Gravity, Sediment and Water, and Sulfur content will be performed by the DOE M&O contracted
third party inspector. Contractor shall have the right to have a representative present to witness all sampling, measurements and testing analysis.
The DOE M&O contracted third party inspection company will witness all measurement and testing and perform testing if necessary. There is no
adjustment to the fixed premium of this contract. All other provisions of the agreement remain the same.
Except as provided herein, all terms and conditions of the document referenced in Item 9A or 10A, as heretofore changed, remains unchanged and in full force and effect.
