The government just lent out another 16.75 million barrels from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve. Trading companies took 91.9% of it: Trafigura, Gunvor, Vitol and Macquarie. These are firms that buy and sell oil but do not run U.S. refineries.

Companies took 98.5% of the oil offered, even though the government asked for more extra oil back than in the round before. The oil is due back between October 2028 and December 2029, plus 14% to 19.5% extra.

For the round before this one, we estimated what the trade could pay using futures prices from the day bids were due. We cannot do that for this round yet. We do not have futures prices for its bid day, October 21, that we can check against a second source.

So here is an ask. If you have access to the WTI forward curve for October 21, including the 2028 and 2029 contracts, let us know how much these traders are likely to make on this. Send me a message or reply in the comments.
