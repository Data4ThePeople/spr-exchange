# Hero image prompts: Sell It or Lend It? With Our Emergency Oil, the Devil Is in the Details

Target: 1680x1080 landscape. Generate wide (16:9 or 3:2); the crop is done with
`~/.claude/d4tp-process/hero fit`. Keep the barrel, the two papers and the
magnifying glass inside the middle 80% of the frame so the crop does not clip
them.

Concept (Claude's, October 13, 2026, for Eric to accept or replace): the
post's theme is "the devil is in the details," and the detail is whether the
government sells its emergency oil or lends it. So: the same navy blue
STRATEGIC PETROLEUM RESERVE barrel from the first post's hero, now sitting
beside two contracts, one stamped SOLD and one stamped LENT, with a magnifying
glass over the fine print. In the fine print, a small red devil's tail or
horns shows through the glass.

Rules for every variant: no recognizable person, no party symbols, no company
names or logos, no real federal seal. The devil is a small cartoon detail
hidden in the fine print, the way the saying means it. It is not a person, it
is not near any name, and nothing in the image ties it to a company, an agency
or an official.

## Prompt 1

Photorealistic wide shot of a plain government-style office desk under a desk
lamp, dark wood, seen from slightly above. On the left of the desk stands one
steel oil barrel in miniature, about the size of a coffee can, painted navy
blue, dented, with white stenciled capital letters on three lines:
STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. In front of it lie two thick paper contracts
side by side, each many pages, held with binder clips. The left contract has a
large red rubber stamp across its cover that reads only SOLD. The right
contract has a large blue rubber stamp across its cover that reads only LENT.
A brass magnifying glass rests on the right contract. The body text of both
contracts is tiny gray fine print, too small to read. Inside the circle of the
magnifying glass, the fine print is enlarged but still unreadable, and
curling out from between two lines of it is one small bright red pointed
devil's tail, drawn simply, like a doodle hidden in the text. Shallow depth of
field, warm lamp light, sharp focus on the magnifying glass and the barrel.
35mm documentary style, high detail. No other readable text, no watermark, no
people.

## Prompt 2 (variant, closer, reads better small)

Photorealistic close-up of a magnifying glass held over a page of dense gray
fine print on a paper contract, the print too small to read. Inside the glass,
the enlarged lines of print are still unreadable, and two tiny bright red
devil horns and a pointed red tail poke out from behind one line of text, as
if something small were hiding in the paragraph. The glass fills the right
half of the frame. In the left half, slightly out of focus, a navy blue steel
oil barrel stands on the desk with white stenciled capital letters on three
lines: STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. Between the barrel and the glass, the
corners of two other pages show two rubber stamps, one red reading only SOLD
and one blue reading only LENT. Warm desk lamp light, dark wood desk, 35mm,
high detail. No other readable text, no watermark, no people, no hands with
identifying features.

## Prompt 3 (variant, full-size barrel)

Photorealistic wide shot inside a bare concrete warehouse. One full-size navy
blue steel oil barrel stands upright in the middle of the floor under a single
overhead light, with white stenciled capital letters on three lines:
STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. Two paper tags hang from the top rim of the
barrel on strings, like price tags: a red tag that reads only SELL and a blue
tag that reads only LEND. Leaning against the barrel is a very long printed
contract that unrolls across the floor toward the camera like a scroll,
covered in tiny gray fine print too small to read. A large magnifying glass
lies on the scroll in the foreground, and inside the glass, among the enlarged
unreadable print, sits one small bright red pointed devil's tail curling out
from between the lines. 35mm documentary style, natural color, sharp focus on
the barrel and the glass, high detail. No other readable text, no watermark,
no people.

## Notes for generating

- The most common failure is misspelled lettering. Check the three words on
  the barrel and the two stamp or tag words letter by letter. If the spelling
  is wrong, regenerate, or ask for blank surfaces and add the words afterward
  in an image editor.
- If the fine print comes back as readable words or fake legal sentences, add
  "fine print is illegible gray lines, no readable words."
- If the devil comes back as a full figure, a face or a person in costume, add
  "only a small red pointed tail hidden between lines of text, no face, no
  figure, no person."
- If a seal, flag or agency name appears, add "no seals, no flags, no agency
  names, no logos."
- Prompt 1 is the closest to the concept and carries the barrel over from the
  first post. Prompt 2 reads best at thumbnail size because the magnifying
  glass is large in the frame.
