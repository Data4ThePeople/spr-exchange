# Hero image prompts: Meet the Speculative Petroleum Reserve

Target: 1680x1080 landscape. Generate wide (16:9 or 3:2); the crop is done with
`~/.claude/d4tp-process/hero fit`. Keep the barrel and the podium inside the
middle 80% of the frame so the crop does not clip them.

Eric's concept: a fancy auction house, but the lot on display is a barrel of
crude oil instead of a car or a watch. The barrel reads "Strategic Petroleum
Reserve" and the podium carries a U.S. government emblem.

Rules for every variant: no real auction house name or logo, no recognizable
person, no party symbols, no flags other than a plain U.S. flag if one appears.
The emblem on the podium is a generic eagle-and-shield government-style seal
with no readable lettering, not the real seal of the Energy Department or the
Great Seal of the United States.

## Prompt 1

Photorealistic wide shot of the sale room in an elegant, old-money auction
house. In the center of the frame, on a low velvet-draped plinth under a soft
spotlight, stands a single steel oil barrel painted deep navy blue, clean and
polished like a museum piece. Stenciled on the side of the barrel in large
white capital letters, on three lines: STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. To the
right of the barrel, an auctioneer in a dark suit stands behind a dark wooden
podium with a gavel raised, seen at three-quarter angle so the face is turned
away and not recognizable. On the front of the podium is a round gold emblem
in the style of a government seal, with an eagle and a shield and no readable
text. In the foreground, out of focus, the backs of seated bidders in formal
clothes, several holding up numbered white paddles. Walnut paneling, brass
picture lights, deep red carpet, warm light. 35mm, shallow depth of field,
sharp focus on the barrel, high detail. No other text anywhere in the image,
no watermark.

## Prompt 2 (variant, no people facing the camera)

Photorealistic view from the back row of a grand auction room, looking over
the shoulders of bidders toward the stage. Raised white bidding paddles fill
the lower third of the frame, slightly blurred. On the stage, a single navy
blue steel oil barrel sits on a rotating display plinth under a bright
spotlight, with a red velvet rope in front of it. White stenciled capital
letters on the barrel read, on three lines: STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE.
Beside it stands an empty dark wooden podium with a gavel resting on top and a
round gold government-style emblem on its front, an eagle and a shield with no
readable lettering. Behind the stage, a tall dark curtain. Rich, warm, low
light with the spotlight as the brightest point. Cinematic, 35mm, high detail,
no other text, no watermark.

## Prompt 3 (variant, closer)

Photorealistic close view of a navy blue steel oil barrel on a velvet-covered
display stand in an auction house, lit by one spotlight from above so the
metal shows soft highlights. White stenciled capital letters on the barrel
read, on three lines: STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. A small white lot card
on a brass stand sits in front of the barrel, blank. In the background, out of
focus, a wooden auctioneer's podium with a round gold eagle-and-shield emblem
and a gavel, and the dim shapes of a seated audience with raised paddles.
Warm wood, brass and deep red tones. 50mm, shallow depth of field, sharp focus
on the barrel lettering, high detail, no other text, no watermark.

## Notes for generating

- The most common failure is misspelled lettering on the barrel. Check all
  three words letter by letter. If the spelling is wrong, regenerate, or ask
  for the barrel with no lettering and add the three words afterward in an
  image editor.
- If the emblem comes back with readable words or looks like a real federal
  seal, add "plain eagle and shield emblem, no words, not an official seal"
  and regenerate.
- If the room's signage shows a real auction house name, add "no signage, no
  brand names" and regenerate.
- Prompt 1 is the closest to the concept. Prompt 3 reads best at thumbnail
  size because the barrel lettering is larger.
