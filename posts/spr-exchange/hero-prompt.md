# Hero image prompts: Meet the Speculative Petroleum Reserve

Target: 1680x1080 landscape. Generate wide (16:9 or 3:2); the crop is done with
`~/.claude/d4tp-process/hero fit`. Keep the barrel and the auctioneer's stand
inside the middle 80% of the frame so the crop does not clip them.

Eric's concept (October 11, 2026): a used car auction, except that instead of
a car, the thing being sold is a barrel of oil. This replaces his earlier
fancy auction house idea and matches the "used car auction" line in the post's
intro. Carried over from the earlier idea: the barrel reads "Strategic
Petroleum Reserve" and the auctioneer's stand carries a U.S. government
emblem.

Rules for every variant: no real auction company name or logo, no car brand
badges, no recognizable person, no party symbols. The emblem is a generic
eagle-and-shield government-style seal with no readable lettering, not the
real seal of the Energy Department or the Great Seal of the United States.

## Prompt 1

Photorealistic wide shot inside a busy wholesale used car auction, the kind
held in a big metal warehouse with a concrete floor and fluorescent lights. A
painted auction lane runs through the middle of the building, with yellow
lines on the floor and a large hanging sign that says only "LANE 1". Where a
car would normally be sitting in the lane, there is instead one single steel
oil barrel, painted navy blue, standing upright on the concrete under the
lights. Stenciled on the side of the barrel in large white capital letters, on
three lines: STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. A paper lot number sticker is
stuck on the barrel the way one is stuck on a car windshield. Above and behind
the lane is a raised wooden auctioneer's booth, where an auctioneer in a polo
shirt leans into a microphone with one arm pointing out at the crowd, face
turned away. On the front of the booth is a round gold eagle-and-shield
emblem with no readable words. Around the lane stand a crowd of car dealers
in work jackets and ball caps, seen from behind and from the side, some
raising a hand or a bidder card. In the far background, rows of ordinary used
cars wait their turn. Documentary photo style, 35mm, natural color, sharp
focus on the barrel, high detail. No other text, no watermark.

## Prompt 2 (variant, seen from the crowd)

Photorealistic photo taken from inside the crowd at a used car auction,
looking over the shoulders of dealers in ball caps toward the auction lane.
Two of them hold up bidder cards, blurred in the foreground. In the lane, in
sharp focus, one navy blue steel oil barrel sits alone on the concrete floor
between the yellow lane lines, exactly where a car would be, with a windshield
style lot number sticker on it. White stenciled capital letters on the barrel
read, on three lines: STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. Behind it, a raised
auctioneer's booth with a microphone, a digital bid board showing only
numbers, and a round gold eagle-and-shield emblem on the front with no
readable words. A line of used cars with their headlights on waits behind the
barrel for the next turn. Big warehouse, open garage doors, daylight coming in
from behind. Documentary style, 35mm, high detail, no other text, no
watermark.

## Prompt 3 (variant, outdoor lot, closer)

Photorealistic photo of an outdoor used car auction lot on a sunny day, with
rows of used cars and strings of colored pennant flags overhead. In the front
row, parked in a marked space between two ordinary sedans, sits one navy blue
steel oil barrel, in sharp focus and filling about a third of the frame.
White stenciled capital letters on the barrel read, on three lines:
STRATEGIC / PETROLEUM / RESERVE. A bright orange price-style tag hangs from
the top of the barrel, blank. Behind it, slightly out of focus, an
auctioneer stands on a small platform with a microphone and a raised hand,
face turned away, next to a stand with a round gold eagle-and-shield emblem
with no readable words. A few bidders stand nearby with their backs to the
camera. 35mm, natural light, high detail, no other text, no watermark.

## Notes for generating

- The most common failure is misspelled lettering on the barrel. Check all
  three words letter by letter. If the spelling is wrong, regenerate, or ask
  for the barrel with no lettering and add the three words afterward in an
  image editor.
- If the scene comes back as a fancy auction room or a car showroom, add
  "wholesale dealer auto auction, warehouse, concrete floor, not a showroom"
  and regenerate.
- If the emblem has readable words or looks like a real federal seal, add
  "plain eagle and shield emblem, no words, not an official seal."
- If cars show brand badges or the building shows a real auction company's
  name, add "no brand names, no logos, no signage other than LANE 1."
- Prompt 1 is the closest to the concept. Prompt 3 reads best at thumbnail
  size because the barrel is larger in the frame.
